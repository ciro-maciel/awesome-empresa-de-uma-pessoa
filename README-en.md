## For thinking about the business

People who have run a small, profitable business, and how.

- [Getting Real](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-getting-real) - Basecamp's book on building less, faster and with a small team, open on the website. `free`
- [MAKE](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-make) - Pieter Levels' book on creating and selling products on your own, without investors. `paid`
- [Pieter Levels no podcast de Lex Fridman](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-pieter-levels-no-podcast-de-lex-fri) - The transcript of the conversation about the companies he runs on his own. `free`
- [Cointeligência](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-cointeligencia) - Ethan Mollick on working with AI as a colleague. Intrinseca. `paid`

## AI agents

What does the work of a team.

- [Claude Code](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-claude-code) - Anthropic's agent that works in the terminal and editor, with documentation in Portuguese. `official` `paid`
- [Building effective agents](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-building-effective-agents) - Anthropic's text on when to use a fixed workflow and when to use an agent. `official` `free`
- [Writing tools for agents](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-writing-tools-for-agents) - How to write tools that an agent uses well, by Anthropic. `official` `free`
- [Model Context Protocol](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-model-context-protocol) - The open protocol that connects agents to tools and data. `free` `open source`
- [CCM Stack](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-ccm-stack) - Plugin for the agent with principles, hooks that refuse the known error, and skills to launch, measure and sell. `open source` `free` `by the author`

## Launch and charge

Site, login, email and payment. In alphabetical order.

- [Cloudflare Pages](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-cloudflare-pages) - Static site hosting with a free plan. `free` `paid`
- [Infrastructure](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-infrastructure) - Hosting, login, storage, payments, email, functions and monitors. Starts free, no card and no time limit, with one feature per product. `free` `paid` `API` `by the author`
- [Resend](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-resend) - Email sending API with a free plan. `free` `paid` `API`
- [Stripe](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-stripe) - Card, Pix and boleto payments in Brazil. `paid` `API`
- [Vercel](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-vercel) - Web application hosting with a free plan. `free` `paid`

## Measure

Who visits, where they came from and what they did.

- [Operations](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-operations) - Analytics without a cookie banner and videos. Starts free, no card and no time limit, with one feature per product. `free` `paid` `by the author`
- [Plausible](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-plausible) - Lightweight, cookie-free analytics, paid or self-hosted. `paid` `open source`
- [Umami](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-umami) - Open source analytics to self-host. `open source` `free`

## Know your customers

Who signed up, who uses it and who pays.

- [Twenty](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-twenty) - Open source CRM. `open source` `free`

- [Decreto do comércio eletrônico](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-decreto-do-comercio-eletronico) - Decree 7.962/2013, for those who sell online. `official` `free`
- [LGPD](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-lgpd) - The General Data Protection Law. `official` `free`
- [WCAG 2.2](https://go.mymarketing.click/umap-wcag-2-2) - The accessibility guidelines for web content. `official` `free`

## Tags

Each item says what it is. The tags next to it help you choose: official when it comes from whoever sets the standard, open source when the code is published, free or paid by usage model, API and SDK by how it integrates. Items tagged "by the author" are made by Ciro Cesar Maciel and follow the same rules as all the others.
